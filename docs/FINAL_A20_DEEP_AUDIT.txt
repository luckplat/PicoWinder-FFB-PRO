ROTATION Z
==========
A19's temporal gate is removed from Z.

Wide center-zero LUT:
- raw 24..38 -> output 32
- raw 0 -> output 0
- raw 23 -> output 31
- raw 39 -> output 33
- raw 63 -> output 63

Outside the center, both sides are monotonically remapped.

A one-count-per-HID-report slew limiter is then applied.
Thus no firmware-generated Z step can exceed 1 count.

- maximum non-zero Z step: 7
